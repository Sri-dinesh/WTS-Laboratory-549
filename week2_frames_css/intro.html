About Vardhaman College of Engineering

Vardhaman College of Engineering is situated in a picturesque landscape providing an optimal academic ambiance. The institution enjoys strategic connectivity via air, rail, and road transit corridors.

Location Advantage: Located 30 minutes from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (Shamshabad) and adjacent to Bangalore National Highway (NH-44).

The campus features modern lecture theaters, high-performance computing centers, dedicated specialized engineering laboratories, and a digital central library. Comprehensive amenities include well-appointed residential facilities, sporting facilities, auditorium, and cafeteria.

High-speed campus-wide connectivity supports progressive pedagogical models, industry workshops, and student-led technological research initiatives.
