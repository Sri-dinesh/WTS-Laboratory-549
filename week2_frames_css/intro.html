About Vardhaman College of Engineering

Vardhaman College of Engineering is surrounded by lush green and beautiful landscape that makes the learning environment pleasant, inspiring, and comfortable. The campus enjoys a prime geographical advantage — strategically connected by air, rail, and major road networks.

Connectivity: The Rajiv Gandhi Hyderabad International Airport at Shamshabad is just a 30-minute drive, and the campus is located adjacent to the Bangalore National Highway (NH-44 / NH-7).

On the academic front, we provide state-of-the-art multimedia classrooms, advanced computing and engineering laboratories, and a digital central library. The campus boasts well-furnished residential hostels, sports complex, gymkhana, auditorium, hygienic cafeteria, and round-the-clock health center.

With high-speed gigabit Wi-Fi across the entire campus, students engage in experiential learning, special guest lectures from industry leaders, national hackathons, and holistic personality development programs.
